# Instructor Key — Practical 14 Threat Intelligence Report Preparation

Expected finding: The output student_report.md contains the timeline and mock feed values, with sections to complete.

Discussion focus: Generate a report starter and revise it into an evidence-based final intelligence report.

Extension check: Complete confidence, competing explanations, impact, and recommendations; cite event IDs.

Do not treat simulated confidence scores as calibrated probabilities. Students should cite the dataset, distinguish analyst inference from a direct log observation, and explain why the result has limits.
