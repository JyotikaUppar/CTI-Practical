# Instructor Key — Practical 7 MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

Expected finding: E02 -> T1059.001 observed; E04 -> T1547.001 observed; E01 -> T1566.001 candidate; E09 -> T1041 hypothesis.

Discussion focus: Assign technique IDs to logged behaviors, attach evidence IDs, and distinguish observation from hypothesis.

Extension check: Find why E10 is intentionally excluded from the C2 mapping; write one alternative explanation for E09.

Do not treat simulated confidence scores as calibrated probabilities. Students should cite the dataset, distinguish analyst inference from a direct log observation, and explain why the result has limits.
