# Instructor Key — Practical 12 Threat Hunting Simulation

Expected finding: At 30 minutes WS-25 links E02 to E05-E09; at one minute it produces no hit.

Discussion focus: Test a host/time correlation hypothesis, then narrow the window to seek a negative result.

Extension check: Write a falsification plan and list missing telemetry.

Do not treat simulated confidence scores as calibrated probabilities. Students should cite the dataset, distinguish analyst inference from a direct log observation, and explain why the result has limits.
