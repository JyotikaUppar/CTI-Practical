# Instructor Key — Practical 9 Threat Actor Profiling

Expected finding: UNATTRIBUTED-01 is a tracking label; there is insufficient evidence for a named group.

Discussion focus: Write a provisional activity profile grounded in events, with explicit limits on attribution.

Extension check: Draft an alternative explanation for the same TTPs and list evidence needed to test it.

Do not treat simulated confidence scores as calibrated probabilities. Students should cite the dataset, distinguish analyst inference from a direct log observation, and explain why the result has limits.
